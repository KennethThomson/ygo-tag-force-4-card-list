# Yu-Gi-Oh! 5D's Tag Force 4 — Card List

**→ [kenneththomson.github.io/ygo-tag-force-4-card-list](https://kenneththomson.github.io/ygo-tag-force-4-card-list/)**

A searchable, filterable database of every card in **Yu-Gi-Oh! 5D's Tag Force 4**
(PSP, 2009), including **how to obtain each one**: which booster pack, at what rarity,
what unlocks that pack, and which cards come only from Tank, the story, a Konami
download or UMD Recognition.

## Dataset

**4,062 cards** (the game's wiki page says 4,063; one card could not be identified).
48 booster packs, three Forbidden/Limited lists (March 2009, September 2009, March 2010),
Dark Synchro and Dark Tuner monsters.

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Card list, pack contents, rarity, prices, unlock conditions | Yugipedia booster pack pages |
| Card stats and text | `(Tag Force 4)` pages where they exist, otherwise the earlier Tag Force games' or the general card page |
| Forbidden/Limited lists | Yugipedia |
| Non-pack sources | Yugipedia (Tank, downloads, UMD Recognition, initial deck) plus GameFAQs walkthroughs for story-reward cards |
| Card art | In-game card-info screenshots from Tag Force 4, falling back to Tag Force 3, 2, 1, 5, then Duel Links, then the TCG print |

## Known gaps

- One card of the wiki's 4,063 is unidentified and not listed.
- Pack contents and unlocks follow the wiki; a few GameFAQs-guide discrepancies were not resolved.
